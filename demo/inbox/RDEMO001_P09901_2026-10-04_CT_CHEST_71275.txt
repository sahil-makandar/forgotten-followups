CLINICAL HISTORY
62-year-old female, former smoker, presenting with chest pain. Rule out pulmonary embolism.

TECHNIQUE
CT pulmonary angiogram with intravenous contrast.

FINDINGS
No pulmonary embolism. There is a 9 x 9 mm solid pulmonary nodule in the right upper lobe, not seen on prior imaging. No lymphadenopathy. No pleural effusion. The heart is normal in size.

IMPRESSION
1. No pulmonary embolism.
2. Incidental 9 x 9 mm solid right upper lobe nodule. Follow-up CT chest is recommended in 3 months.
